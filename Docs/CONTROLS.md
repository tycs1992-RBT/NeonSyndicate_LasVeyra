# Controls

Keyboard and mouse, or a game controller (Xbox, PlayStation, Switch Pro and most others). The game switches the moment you touch either one, and button prompts follow the device you used last. Click the game window once to capture the mouse. **F1** in game shows a quick reference.

Gadgets (turret, homing mine, hover drone, shield, scanner, vapor launcher) run on cooldowns, not crystal energy. While a gadget sits in slot 1 or 2, the number key 1 or 2 uses it instead of picking the melee weapon or the handgun (the mouse wheel, the weapon wheel and keys 3 to 8 still switch weapons). On a controller a quick tap of D-pad left or right still switches weapons; holding it past 0.3 s starts the gadget. Self-cast gadgets (drone, handheld shield, Ripple scanner) work the moment you let go. Full guide: `Docs/GADGETS.md`.

Fights are deliberate: enemies flash and glow before they swing, so watch for the wind-up, then roll through it or parry. Heavy hitters can be staggered by breaking their poise. Rolling has a stamina cost; guarding drains stamina per hit and an empty bar breaks your guard.

Aim a gun at a store clerk to rob the register. Aim at a driver and they may bail out or floor it.

Travel powers are free to use and work inside the Veil. They work while you are wanted (police cars and helicopters still chase you). They cannot be used in vehicles, in cutscenes or while knocked down. Leaps, glides and flights never cause fall damage. A Surge knocks pedestrians aside without a wanted level, but plowing into police counts as attacking an officer.

## Crystal powers

| Action | Keyboard | Controller |
|---|---|---|
| Third Eye on or off (bullet time) | Tap Caps Lock or T | Tap LB + RB together |
| Shift through the Veil into the Inner World, or back out | Hold T or Caps Lock for 0.7 s | Hold LB + RB for 0.7 s |
| Cast the selected power | B | LB + RT together |
| Choose a power | [ and ] | Tap D-pad up for the next power |
| Scan a creature for the bestiary | With the Third Eye on, keep it near the middle of the screen (within 35 m) for 1.2 s | Same, with the right stick |

Powers use the crystal bar. It refills over time and with takedowns. The Focus attribute and gear with power affixes make them hit harder.

Gun hits on hostiles refill the crystal bar too, and attack powers leave a glowing mark on what they hit: shoot a marked foe for a Resonance Burst (no new buttons; see Docs/COMBAT_LOOP.md). Floating damage numbers can be turned off in Pause > Settings.

A scan fills the creature's BESTIARY entry (pause menu tab: weak point, element, drops), pays Lumen the first time for each species and marks its weak point for 20 s. Creatures you only see within 40 m get a basic entry. The BESTIARY tab works with the mouse and with the D-pad or left stick plus A (Cross); its MATERIAL SATCHEL button lists the materials creatures dropped. See Docs/CREATURES.md.

The Veil opens once the Third Eye is unlocked. Entering costs 25 crystal energy and the Inner World drains 2 per second (less with the Veil skills and perks; the Third Eye's bullet time drains on top, and the bar does not refill there); when it runs dry you are thrown back with a stagger. A ring of dots around the screen centre fills while you hold. Inside the Veil the hold always works, even before the Third Eye is unlocked, so you can always get back out. On macOS with the classic Input Manager, Caps Lock can only tap, so hold T. See Docs/ZONES.md.

## Resonance Shrines

Glowing monoliths (the omega symbol on the map) at hospitals, clinics and safehouses. Use one to rest (refills health and tonics), level up with Lumen, upgrade gear, fast travel between shrines you have lit, and reset your skill trees for Lumen (the SKILLS tab). Dying drops your unspent Lumen as an echo where you fell; reach it before you die again to take it back. A small shrine forge stands beside each shrine.

## Wanted level

- Crimes police see raise your heat (shown as crystals). Civilians who see a crime phone it in; stop them and the call never happens.
- Break line of sight and leave the flashing search area on the minimap until the heat clears.
- A respray clears your heat if no officer is watching.
- At one crystal, an unarmed suspect standing still can be arrested.
- Officer Valez (phone, later in the story) makes warrants disappear for $1,000 a crystal.

## Crystal Discovery (02G)

Ground shrines marked D use the existing interact action: E on keyboard, Y on the active controller layout (north face button). Read MEMORY CORE, walk to SUN / ROOT / STAR in the revealed order and use each, then return to the core to claim. No new key binding is installed. Pause > DISCOVERY lists progress, inscriptions and restored memories. Its Track button sets a normal player waypoint without replacing a mission target. Use the existing SAVE / LOAD menu to retain progress. Wrong rune presses only reset that shrine sequence.

## 04G recovery and delivery notes

Existing keys and pad actions are unchanged. An attack queued during a committed swing waits for its legal recovery. The final cut of a light chain completes its recovery before the next chain. Maul rolls and pursuit attacks have their own slower attacks. A dodge pressed within 0.18 seconds of legal recovery is retained; it never cancels the active hit or extends invulnerability. A held guard rises at that weapon's legal defensive recovery. Clearly telegraphed NPC crystal lances can be perfectly deflected with guard.

For A Light Carried Home, use the existing interact action at the parcel and the Pressure House fuse cabinet. Remain within reach until the fitting locks. Damage or entering a vehicle drops the visible parcel at a recoverable marker. The journal and map point to the current parcel or destination. Saving in the carry phase resumes from the Dispatch pickup; saving after delivery resumes at the report phase. No additional key or controller chord was introduced.

### 04H: The Quiet Circuit

The physical tuning nodes reuse **E / north face button (Y or Triangle)**. Listen until the prompt says **QUIET BEAT**, then interact once. Three different quiet beats align a node; pressing early loses no progress. Leaving pauses the rhythm. A hit disturbs the current node. The existing companion exploration assist can steady one seal per node; a companion is optional. No new binding or controller chord is installed.

## 04O crystal city preview

Title screen > Crystal city preview (04O) starts a separate Free Roam in the revised parcel layout. It reloads the existing game scene. Legacy city Free Roam returns to the original layout. Normal New Game and Start After the Prologue retain the original story layout. Loading a save selects the layout recorded by that save; saves without a revision use the original city. Cross-layout migration is disabled.

In the preview, on-foot exploration can use a crystal compass in the original minimap area. It names a Spine landmark or the existing GPS target and gives direction, distance and floor guidance. M or the existing controller map binding still opens the full map. Vehicles, active wanted searches and an expanded minimap retain the road map. Pause > WORLD switches between the compass and the original road minimap with the existing mouse, keyboard or controller menu navigation. The preference is saved. Existing movement, powers, melee, guns, Resonance, interaction and Pause bindings are unchanged.
