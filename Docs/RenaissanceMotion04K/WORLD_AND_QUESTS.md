## Vanguard: The Fractured Bell

ID `uh05_04k_01_fractured_bell`, optional Underheart side quest, no prerequisite bypass or required bond. Track it in JOURNAL so Corin offers it at Dispatch. Seven phases, 23 steps, six checkpoint resume points:

| Phase | Actual work |
|---|---|
| brief | Speak to Corin, enter Dispatch, read receiver A |
| archive | Enter Archive, inspect manifest, carry physical trace to Pressure House, speak to Tamsin |
| stores | Descend real service stair via Refuge, travel to workshop stores, clear declared four mites + one Hollow |
| relay | Observe garden habitat; manual or earned-companion relay assist; return to Refuge and align three quiet beats |
| champion | Reach workshop convoy passage, explicitly challenge the grounded Fractured Bell, defeat the single finite foe |
| recover | Inspect physical cache, record durable caster claim, carry recovered trace through the stair to Pressure House |
| report | Speak to Tamsin and Corin, physically exit Dispatch; ordinary once-only completion payout |

Completion: $240 cash, 80 Lumen, eight shard chunks, three coolant salt and four glass chitin through existing QuestBook reward logic. The separate caster token is persistent and once-only. No GiveGun step, free bond, evolution/fusion grant, six-focus unique, story flag or main-story skip.

Unlocked paid Forge treatments: `brew_vanguard_edge_04k` costs $130 + four shards + one salt for 360 s, melee +10% and guard stamina cost +6%; `brew_bell_ward_04k` costs $130 + four glass chitin + one salt for 360 s, incoming damage -7% and stamina recovery -5%. Existing buff/save/payment owners remain. No free craft and no overwritten 04H/04I recipe.

Failure/recovery: normal death/abandon/load releases mission/skirmish owners. An arena refusal retries five times then fails recoverably. Missing finite runtime refuses every 04K fight rather than spawning an unconfigured fallback. Lost or failed actors are never victory. The existing persistent flag skips a confirmed won champion; Claim receipts prevent another caster. Full inventory/missing systems leave a pending or queued entitlement. Restoring an earlier checkpoint may repeat unpaid interaction/travel work and the rewardless store swarm. This must still be tested natively across quit/death/load/abandon/scene switch.

30–60 minutes is a target only; no stopwatch playtest was possible. The exact corridor fit, doors, journal prompts, companion optional path and reward retrieval remain native-unverified.
