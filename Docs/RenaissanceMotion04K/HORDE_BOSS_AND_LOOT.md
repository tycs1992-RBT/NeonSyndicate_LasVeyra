# Finite swarm, Fractured Bell, and actual loot policy

The new quest declares four Glass Mites and one Hollow, followed later by one elite Quartz Drake. It uses existing UnderheartEcology.BeginSkirmish, arena admission, finite ledger, attack tokens, safe post placement, loss/death handling and cleanup. No second encounter director or infinite reinforcement loop exists. New quest actors suppress ordinary kill/part/material loot; the reward belongs to the saved quest ledger, preventing abandonment farming.

The Fractured Bell is a runtime option of canonical `quartz_drake`, not a new saved species or owned BONDS entry. A fixed existing convoy-corridor post avoids radial placement outside its narrow floor. Health scale 0.12 x elite 2.5 x original 2,400 = 720 HP; damage scale 0.35 retains existing difficulty/attack tuning. Its flight flag is disabled. It retains real bite/claw/tail/breath/slam actions and Frost weakness. Initial windups are at least 1.05 s; at <=45% HP at least 0.85 s. Recovery remains >=1.3/1.05 s respectively, exposing incoming damage x1.35 only in recovery. Between attacks at least 1.1 s rest applies. There is no immunity phase or new extra damage volume. These are source rules, not measured gameplay.

A confirmed finite victory writes existing QuestBook flag `uh05_04k_bell_defeated`. Retry/reload can skip the won fight. Failed/vanished/aborted encounters cannot set it; a post-loop failure check also closes an older success-on-failed-Done risk. The recovered Epic CrystalCaster is `uh05_04k_recovered_caster`, a deterministic normal ledger gun with no forced unique index. Full inventory leaves it pending; regrant and repeated claim do not duplicate delivery in the external transaction test. Physical pickup of ordinary earned 04J world drops remains native-unverified.

04J's ambient caster rarity policy is preserved in CrystalLootPolicy04K and called by the actual DropRolled hook: random Mythic caster -> Legendary; explicit unique rewards and non-casters retain rarity. The inherited test incorrectly assumed the catalog had no general Mythic caster; an actual rerun found index 15. The corrected seeded test exercises the runtime cap, all five rolled rarities, caster type/element/save record and no entitlement-only foci 17–22. The old item catalog/indices and UID format are unchanged.
