# Underheart and Bonds 04E: art and performance

## Asset inventory and licences

04E adds **no binary art or audio**. Every mesh, material, texture and sound is generated by C# at runtime, like the rest
of the game, so there is nothing to license and no LFS object was added.

| Content | Source |
|---|---|
| Underheart rooms, stairs, catwalks, lava channels, vents, lifts | `World/Underheart/UnderheartBuilder*.cs` and friends (procedural meshes, `Mats`) |
| New interiors (Cinder Dispatch, Pressure House, Bondkeeper's Refuge, Jax's bay) | venue layouts in `Resources` plus `ArtInhabitedCity04C/ArrivalScene04E*.cs` |
| Companions (drakeling, warden, skimmer) and their rank looks | `Creatures/Companion*.cs` (`CompanionSculpt`, one skinned mesh, one draw call) |
| Fused forms and trims | `Vehicles/Fusion/FusionFormGeometry*.cs`, `FusionArt.cs` |
| Underground species and bosses | `CreatureBuilder` plans, `Creatures/UnderBosses/*` |
| Sounds and loops | `Audio/Underheart04ESounds*.cs` (synthesized) |

## Budgets in source (not measured)

- The Underheart builds only the chosen variant's cells; rooms report `UNDERHEART_04E built rooms=... colliders=...
  renderers=...` in the log so a native run can record the real counts.
- Companions: one skinned mesh each, one active at a time. A fused form hides the companion body.
- Boss adds are bounded per arena and bound to it; the encounter slot allows one encounter panel at a time.
- A review flagged that the Kiln Regent arena may exceed the light budget. **Not measured and not changed.**
