# The Underheart: world map (04E)

The four new interiors (anchors, staff posts, clearances) are documented in `VENUES.md`; this page covers entrances, regions, depths and shortcuts.

The Underheart is a real, walkable network of rooms, tunnels and caverns built under Las Veyra at runtime. Nothing is
loaded from disk: the layout is plain maths (`World/Underheart/UnderheartLayout*.cs`), the geometry is generated by
`UnderheartBuilder*`, and `UnderheartWorld*` runs it (regions, heat, shortcuts, rest points, map, routing, saves).

This page is the reference for what is down there, where, and why. Everything below was checked offline (compile and
EditMode tests). Nothing here has been seen in the Unity editor yet: see "What to check in Unity" at the end.

## How you get in

1. **The Pressure House stair (the first entrance, on foot).** The stair starts inside the Pressure House (a venue the
   VENUES stream adds to the inhabited city) at the floor opening the venue leaves for it: venue-local x 4.3 to 7.1,
   z 3.0 to 14.6. It drops 6.15 m in 31 risers of 0.198 m over 11 m (0.355 m treads), from the venue floor (0.15) to
   the service level (floor -6.0). The opening is registered with `WorldApertures` before the city is built, so
   CityBuilder leaves the ground slab, the block platform collider and the lot surface out of it. The Underheart
   builds every tread, the shaft walls through the slab and the basement; VENUES builds the rails above floor level.
2. **The vehicle portal (optional, by car or bike).** At boot a survey looks for an open lot near where the Ember
   Canals transit tunnel runs and cuts a straight ramp into it. Rules: a Park or Plaza lot with no building, no POI,
   not custom built and not a parking lot; at least 20 m on a side; inside the city core; at least 60 m clear of the
   canal channel band; at least 150 m from Veyra Lofts. The 8 m wide cut keeps clear of trees, palms, street lamps,
   props, pickups, collectibles, creature spots, rocks, radio masts, stunt jumps, POIs, special sites, the pedestrian
   walk network, the park path cross, the decorative crystal groves and the lot centre. The cut and ramp slope 18
   degrees (the limit is 20) down to -6.8 m, then a 10 m wide, 7 m high access tunnel (straight runs and corners)
   joins the end of `canals.transit`. A tunnel longer than 560 m counts as no fit. The variant with the shortest
   tunnel wins. If no lot fits, the portal is skipped: the reason is in `Game.Underheart.PortalReason`, the
   Underheart status line and the STATUS page row "Underheart vehicle portal".

Offline survey of the default city (seed 61724): all 38 plausible Pressure House door frames 30 to 160 m from Veyra
Lofts found a portal, with tunnels between 154 and 446 m, all in the Park lot at x -377 to -262, z 162 to 240.

### Layout variants

The deep part of the network (everything below the refuge vestibule) can turn left, right or forward and mirror, so
it fits under any Pressure House placement: six variants (`UnderheartLayout.VariantCount`). `UnderheartPlanner` drops
the variants that would reach the canal channel band (plus 30 m) or the map edge (minus 60 m) and keeps the one with
the shortest vehicle portal tunnel. The same city and venue placement always give the same variant, and saves resolve
anchors by id, so a change of variant never strands a save.

in all six).

Every region (after the service level) has a critical path from its entry room to its exit room inside the region, at
least one optional side room off that path, a landmark, a rest point in a safe room and a doorway to the next region
(the Foundry Crown, the last, has the crown lift instead). The whole network is connected from the stair bottom to
every room and every anchor with every shortcut closed; shortcuts are only ways back. The `Underheart04ETests` check
all of this for all six variants.

**The large vista.** The Foundry Crown balcony looks across the chamber through a wide window cut in the far wall onto
a painted abyss of lit towers and far glow, so the player reads a city-sized system beyond the playable space. The
playable collision stays bounded by the chamber walls.

### Named places

All 91 ids in `UnderheartIds.Anchors` exist: 90 in the layout plus `portal.top` when the vehicle portal is built.
Each anchor has an exact floor point and facing, sits at least 0.4 m from its room's walls, is in the region its
prefix names and never stands in a heat zone. Quest places use `Under:<anchorId>`; `Game.Underheart.TryAnchor` gives
the live position.

## Shortcuts (return paths)

| Id | Kind | Opened at (the deep end) | Returns to |
|---|---|---|---|
| `sc_refuge_lift` | Lift | `refuge.lift` (refuge lift bay) | Service level hall, beside the stair |
| `sc_canals_gate` | Sliding gate | `canals.gate` (valve room) | The stair up to the refuge works (`canals.scbottom`) |
| `sc_workshops_hoist` | Lift | `workshops.hoist` (workshops entry hall) | Ember Canals maintenance hut |
| `sc_crown_lift` | Lift | `crown.lift` (Foundry Crown staging) | Lantern Refuge lift bay |

- A lift is restored with E (pad: interact) at its deep end; after that it rides either way. From the far end a closed
  lift says where to restore it. A gate is unbarred from the valve room; from the stair side it says it is barred.
- **Lifts are short disclosed loads.** A ride is `Game.Root.TravelTo`: a fade out of about 0.45 s, a teleport to the
  other cage, and a 0.6 s fade in. There is no lift shaft geometry between the two cages. The lift refuses to run
  while the player has been hit in the last 3 seconds.
- Opening is persistent (`UnderheartSave.Shortcuts`) and raises `ShortcutOpened(id)` plus a "SHORTCUT OPENED" banner
  (quests can open one with `UnlockShortcut(id, announce)`).

## Rest points

`refuge.rest`, `canals.rest`, `gardens.rest`, `workshops.rest`, `reliquary.rest`, `crown.staging`, all in safe rooms
(cells flagged `Safe`: no damaging heat, no vehicle access; keeping routine hostile spawns out of them is the ECOLOGY
stream's rule). "Press E to rest" heals 35 % of maximum health, puts
out burning, sets `UnderheartSave.RestAnchor` and the safehouse there (`Game.Root.SetSafehouse`) and autosaves when the
game allows it (a running mission blocks autosaves unless a 04E campaign checkpoint says it is safe). Resting again
waits 20 s.

**Rest points and saves.** A rest point is the respawn point underground and the resume point for saves made
underground only. `SetSafehouse` changes `GameRoot.LastSafehouse` but never `GameRoot.LastSurfaceSafehouse` (the last
safehouse used on the surface, or the home spawn when none was). `SaveSystem.Capture` asks
`SafehouseRules04E.SavePosition`: a save made underground (the same test as the Underheart block's `SavedRegion`) keeps
the rest point and loads through the Underheart resume path; a save made on the surface stores the surface safehouse,
so it never loads underground. The surface safehouse is saved too (appended `SaveData.HasSurfaceSafehouse`,
`SurfaceSafeX/Y/Z`), so it survives an underground save. Older saves without those fields use their own surface
safehouse, else the home spawn. A surface save that an earlier 04E build wrote with a rest point (no `SavedRegion`)
resumes at the surface safehouse (`SafehouseRules04E.SurfaceSaveHeldRestPoint`).

**Where you wake up.** Dying or falling underground wakes you at the rest point of the region you were in (once that
region is discovered), else the last rest point you used in that region, else the region's entry (`service.stair_bottom`,
`refuge.entry`, `canals.entry`, `gardens.entry`, `workshops.entry`, `reliquary.entry`, `crown.staging`). A car lost in
a transit tunnel goes back to the nearest vehicle bay (`canals.transit`, `canals.portal_bottom`,
`workshops.convoy_start`, `workshops.convoy_end`), never into a walk-only room.

## Heat

| Kind | Where | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| Lava | Canal trench (34/s), garden seeps and the matron's lair (30/s), workshop slag (28/s), the crown ring (32/s) | Sunken 0.5 m channels (always climbable out of) with striped lips. Damage while the feet are in the channel; jumping over is safe |
| Hot vents | The canal assist route (5 vents, 16/s, 4.2 s cycle), the garden cool route (2, 14/s), the crown chamber (2, 16/s) | Breathe for 55 % of their cycle, out of step with each other; the ring glows brighter as the next breath nears |
| Cooling vents | Between the hot vents, the cool pocket, the garden pool, the crown cooling duct | Blue rings and mist. Standing in one cools you for 6 s: vents stop hurting, lava burns at half rate, burning goes out |
| Warm scenery | Valve housing, crystal clusters, furnaces, the Pyre Tree, the heart core | Looks hot, never hurts |

Damage is Fire through the player's `CharacterHealth`, so wards, aspects and gear apply. One second of grace (a pulsing
HEAT warning in the heat row, `HudLayout04E`) comes first; then a tick every 0.25 s. Burning from lava is capped at 0.5 s so
stepping out ends it. Only the room the player stands in counts: heat never reaches through a ceiling or a wall.
`HazardAt(p, out heatPerSecond)` answers the same question for other systems.

## The vehicle route

`canals.transit` (10 m wide, 7 m high, 80 m long), the `workshops.transitdescent` ramp (down 29 m) and the
`workshops.convoy` corridor are vehicle cells, plus the portal cut, ramp and access tunnel. Every doorway from a
vehicle cell into a walk-only room is either under 1.2 m wide or a chicane (two staggered baffles people step around),
so cars stay out of the refuges and rooms. `VehicleSpaceAt(p, out headroom)` reports where a vehicle may drive or fuse.
A "VEHICLE BAY" sign marks the parking spot in the transit tunnel.

## What else keeps out of the openings

`WorldApertures.Contains(p, margin)` keeps these off the stair and portal openings (small hooks outside the
Underheart files, listed so the integrator can review them):

- `World/CityBuilder.cs`: the ground slab collider and sand tiles, the block platform collider and every street-level
  surface drawn with `TiledRect` (lots, sidewalks, park paths, pads) are built around the openings.
- `Gameplay/LootWorld.cs` (`Exists`): no street-level chest in an opening (1.5 m margin).
- `Gameplay/CrystalArsenal/CrystalEncounters.Site.cs`: no breach site centre within 6 m, no anchor within 1.5 m.
- `Gameplay/Discovery/DiscoveryPlacement.cs` (`Probe`): no discovery point within 2 m.
- `Vehicles/TrafficManager.cs` (`TrySpawnParked`): no parked car within 3 m.
- `World/CityRuntime.cs` (`LightPool`): the street lamp lights rest while the view is underground (they would shine
  through the ground onto the service level).

## Streaming and budgets

- One render group per region, plus `entrance` (the stair) and `portal` (the cut and ramp). The groups of the region
  you are in and its neighbours are on; the stair group (with the service level) shows within 70 m of the Pressure
  House door and the portal group within 450 m of the portal top, from the street or the title flyover camera.
- Colliders are never streamed: every floor, wall, ceiling, tread and gate collider stays on (layer World).
- Practical lights: point lights only, shadowless, created off; every 0.25 s the 8 nearest in the visible groups are
  on (5 on Low). So at most 8 Underheart point lights are ever active. While an Underheart boss fight is armed or
  running and the view is within its arena radius plus 20 m, one fewer (7, or 4 on Low), so the fight's single key
  light keeps the total at 8 (5 on Low); the full budget is back within 0.25 s after the fight ends, fails or resets, or
  the view leaves (`UnderheartLightBudget.cs`, read only from `Game.UnderBosses`).
- Geometry is merged per render group and material (buckets flush at 60 000 vertices). Shells near the street
  (entrance, portal, service level) cast two-sided shadows so sunlight cannot fall through the cut slab; deeper shells
  cast none.
- The boot log line `UNDERHEART_04E built rooms=... colliders=... renderers=... triangles=... lights=... props=...
  prepareMs=... buildMs=...` and the F2 Underheart page give the real numbers.

## Atmosphere

The Atmosphere's underground mode (`World/Underheart/AtmosphereUnderground.cs`) blends in a step below the street:
0.85 on the service level, 1.0 deeper. Sun and moon go out, ambient light and fog take the region's palette (cool teal
in the refuge, ember in the canals, amber in the gardens, sooty iron in the workshops, pale ash in the reliquary,
red-orange in the crown), rain, dust and snow stop and are cleared, reflections dim, thunder and the weather ambience
fall silent. The weather state is never touched, so leaving, dying, loading or the title screen restores the street
on its own. Teleports (lifts, loads, respawns) snap the mood instead of fading it.

## Map and HUD

- Pause MAP: the "UNDERHEART LAYER" button (bottom right; mouse, or the pad's menu cursor and A) cycles AUTO, ON, OFF.
  AUTO shows the layer while you are underground. The layer darkens the street map and draws every room of every
  discovered region tinted by region, deepest first, with rest points (R), lifts (L) and the gate (G) (gold when
  open), landmarks (*), the current route and a hover readout of the room's region, depth and kind. The stair (U)
  and portal (V) entrances always show.
- While underground an opaque panel replaces the minimap: north up, rooms at your level bright and other levels dim,
  icons, the route to the GPS target as dots, region and depth, the clock, and the target's floor.

## Saves

`SaveData.Underheart` (`UnderheartSave`, append-only; version 2 adds `SavedRegion`, `SavedAnchor`, `LayoutVariant`).
An underground save resumes at its exact spot when that spot is in the same layout variant, has floor under it and no
heat; otherwise at the saved fallback anchor (the region's rest point or entry), then the rest point nearest the saved
spot. If the Underheart is unavailable in the loading session, the player wakes at the Pressure House door (or on the
street above) with a notice, and every shortcut, discovery and cleared room is kept. Old saves load with nothing
granted.

## Debug

F2 > Underheart (Order 60): status and build stats, the vehicle portal, the streaming groups, your cell, heat and
return point; discover every region, open or close every shortcut, reset Underheart progress, go to the Pressure
House; go to each region's entry; toggle each shortcut; a paged list of all anchors to teleport to.

## What to check in Unity

These cannot be verified without the editor:

1. Boot log: `UNDERHEART_04E prepare ...` and `UNDERHEART_04E built ...`, the STATUS page rows, and the build time.
2. Walk the stair from the Pressure House floor to the service level: treads under the feet (no sinking or floating),
   no gap at the top or bottom, the shaft walls meeting the venue's floor opening and rails.
3. Look down the stair opening and the vehicle portal from the street: no ground plane, sand tile or lot surface
   across the hole, no sky showing through, rails all round the portal cut.
4. Drive down the portal ramp into the transit tunnel and up again; try to drive into the maintenance hut (blocked).
5. Every region: lighting reads (no black rooms, no blown-out rooms), landmark silhouettes, fog density, the vista
   window from the Foundry Crown balcony.
6. Heat: the HEAT warning, the grace second, vents breathing out of step, blue vents cooling, lava trench depth.
7. Lifts and the gate: restore, ride both ways, the fade, arrival facing out of the cage.
8. Rest, die underground, fall through (F2 teleport off a ledge), save and load underground, load the same save with
   the venue catalog missing (the surface fallback notice).
9. Street lamps at night near the Pressure House: no warm light patches on the service level ceiling.
10. Frame rate in the gardens cavern and the crown chamber (Perf flythrough does not cover the Underheart; use the
    F2 counters and the profiler).
