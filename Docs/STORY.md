# The story of Neon Syndicate: Las Veyra

The main story is twelve missions (`Gameplay/StoryMissions.cs`). This page tells it in plain words: the idea that holds
it together, who is in it, what each chapter does, and the two endings. Every mission id, chapter, contact, place and
core event below matches the code, so saves keep working. Side content (guild quests, bosses, world events) fits the
same story but stays optional.

## The idea in one breath

A father who is not a hero, a daughter who hears another world, and a city that wants to sell her. Everything strange
in Las Veyra (the crystals, the Veil, the creatures, the powers) comes from one broken thing: the Heart of the Crystal
Manor.

## The through-line

**The Crystal Manor was a lock.** Long before Las Veyra had a name, the Manor stood on the seam between the city and
the Veil, the red-violet mirror city on the other side. Its Heart held the wall between the two shut. The Heart broke,
the Manor fell to ruins, and its shards scattered. The wall has been thinning ever since.

**Luma's signal.** Luma was born able to hear the Veil ("the stones singing"). The ruined Manor needs a new keeper, and
it chose her: her signal is the Manor calling her home. When the Third Eye wakes in chapter 1, the signal blooms into a
beacon that can be felt on both sides of the wall.

**Why the Veil is opening.** A signal that strong pulls the thin places apart. Red tears open over bridges, parks and
radio masts, and the Veil leaks into the city: Shard-Touched elites, champions and weapon cores are people and metal
soaked in Veil glass.

**Why creatures are crossing.** The creatures of the Veil follow the beacon through the tears, the way moths follow a
lamp. That is why mites crawl out of storm drains and winged things hit cars over the canal, and why the creatures
started showing up the week Luma came to town. Four great beasts grew around big pieces of the broken Heart: the
Glacier Titan in the Frostline, the Shard Golem Foreman in the quarry, the Mirage Djinn in the desert, and the Mirror
Wraith Queen inside the Veil's own copy of the Manor. They are optional bosses; each one drops its heart material.

**The Shard Warden.** The Manor's last steward, a guardian that fused itself with a shard of the Heart and is now more
crystal than person. It walked out of the Manor before the Manor fell, and has waited ever since. It wants the Third
Eye and Luma's voice, because with both it can call the Heart back together, rebuild the Manor as a throne, close the
Veil under one law (its own) and rule both cities. It believes families make rulers weak. It never comes in person
until the end: it pays hunters (the Glass Row Hunters, the False King, the Shard Wolves), buys information, and funds
it all through the Mirage Lotus.

**Malik's growing bond.** The Third Eye bonds to Malik because of Luma ("FATHER-BOND DETECTED"): it grows stronger
every time he protects her. Every power is that bond showing a new face.

- Chapter 1: the Third Eye wakes and gives him the Sunbolt, bullet time and the way into the Veil (hold the Third Eye).
  Surge (super speed) works from the start.
- After chapter 1: **Super Leap and Glide**. Ion notices "your legs read like springs", and the banner says the bond
  deepens.
- After chapter 2: **Flight**. Luma feels the drive through her signal: "it wants you in the sky".
- The other crystal powers (Ember Chain, Static Lance, Quartz Ward, Mirage Step, Gravity Well, Canal Mend, Roc Call,
  Ghost Frequency, Glass Sight, Drive Sigil) are more facets of the same bond, earned as Malik grows. The ending
  strengthens either the shelter powers (Mercy) or the fighting powers (Power).

**The guilds and factions** are how the city lives with the leak. The Menders (Mara) patch people up after the tears,
the Frequency Order (Iris) walks the Veil for a living, the Forgewrights (Dante) turn shard and Veil glass into gear,
and the Frost Wardens hold the Frostline against what comes down from the snow. The syndicates sell to whoever pays,
and lately that is the Warden.

## The twelve chapters

1. **The Broken Map** (`m00_broken_map`, Luma). Malik wakes in the Crystal Manor ruins with Luma, who says something
   outside knows her name. He finds a cyan light past the broken wall: the Third Eye. It bonds to him through his love
   for her, and Luma's signal blooms. Glass Row Hunters, sent for "the girl who hears the stones", attack. Malik fights,
   rides the hover bike through a hunters' blockade and brings Luma to Mara's clinic. Ion warns the signal is still
   climbing and that the stone is still learning Malik. Super Leap and Glide unlock.
2. **Heat Mirrors** (`m01_heat_mirrors`, Jax Calder). Hiding Luma costs money. Jax wants a Vanta Arrow stolen from a
   Glass Jury lawyer, and warns that things with too many legs started crawling out of the drains the night Malik
   arrived. Malik steals the car and loses the police. Afterwards Luma calls: she felt him on that drive, and the
   stone wants him in the sky. Flight unlocks.
3. **Ghost Lanes** (`m02_ghost_lanes`, Nico Ash). Nico's street race is named for the red lights that show a second
   city under the road. Malik wins, and Nico tells him the ghost lights chased him all the way: the Veil knows him.
4. **False Kings** (`m03_false_kings`, Dante Rook). The Brass Tower Union's new king is a hunter in disguise, asking
   about a man with a glowing eye and a girl who hears stones. Malik tails him and ends the reign. The king's last order
   is to bring the stone to the Warden, and Dante confirms the Warden is real: it walked out of the Manor before it fell.
5. **The Flooded Grid** (`m04_flooded_grid`, Mara Solane). A red tear opened over the Sunken Row bridges and something
   with wings knocked Mara's crystal courier into the canal. Malik drives the ambulance down, fights off the Canal Choir
   and brings the couriers back alive. Mara says Luma is drawing crystal paths and a red city under theirs: the Manor is
   talking to her.
6. **Ashline Treaty** (`m05_ashline_treaty`, June Pyre). With creatures coming out of the tears, June wants a truce
   between the Ember Court and the Brass Tower Union. The Court springs an ambush instead, and boasts that the Warden pays
   for every bearer they burn. Malik protects June; the Court becomes a sworn enemy.
7. **Nocturne Market** (`m06_nocturne_market`, Iris Moon). The Red Moth Syndicate is selling a drive holding a
   recording of Luma's signal. Malik chases down the courier. Iris reads the drive: a map of the Manor, a buyer called
   the Shard Warden, and four great beasts sleeping on pieces of the old Heart (ice, glass, sand and a queen of mirrors).
8. **Glass Saints** (`m07_glass_saints`, Officer Valez). Three Glass Jury lawyers keep the Warden's hunters out of every
   cell. Valez gives Malik six minutes of police blindness to remove them.
9. **The Signal War** (`m08_signal_war`, Ion Solane). Static Saints transmitters in Grave Frequency scan for Luma's
   signal and sell it to the Warden, and every scan rips the Veil wider. Malik blows up all three; Luma goes quiet and
   the tears over Grave Frequency start to close.
10. **Crownless Mile** (`m09_crownless_mile`, Sable Knox). The Lotus Casino's armored truck carries two million, and it
    is the Warden's payroll. Malik stops it and drives it to Jax's garage, where Sable double-crosses him because the
    Warden pays better. Malik survives it; the hunters miss payday.
11. **The Long Switch** (`m10_long_switch`, Mara Solane). With its payroll gone, the Warden takes what it wants: Shard
    Wolves snatch Luma and race for the airstrip so the Warden has her before dawn. Malik chases the van, beats the Wolf
    Seeker and frees her. Luma finally explains: the Manor calls her because it needs a keeper, that is her signal, and
    that is why the Veil keeps opening. The Warden wants her voice to bring the Heart back and rule both cities. They
    hide at the Starlite Motel until sunrise.
12. **Sunrise Debt** (`m11_sunrise_debt`, Luma). At dawn the Manor wakes on both sides of the wall. The Shard Warden
    waits at the ruins, shielded by three crystal anchors, and demands the girl's voice so the Veil closes under its law.
    Mara and Ion stand with Malik; he breaks the anchors, defeats the Warden and claims the Manor Heart. The Third Eye
    reports "FATHER-BOND COMPLETE" and asks for the Manor's first law.

## The endings

The Heart can hold the wall between Las Veyra and the Veil again, but it needs a law.

- **Mercy.** The Manor becomes a shelter and the Veil an open door instead of a wound. Mara: "Then we rebuild. One room
  at a time." Luma hears the stones stop crying. Quartz Ward and Canal Mend grow stronger. "The city wanted a monster.
  It got a neighbor."
- **Power.** The Manor becomes a throne and the Veil is sealed under Malik's rule, which is exactly what the Warden
  wanted, now in a father's hands. Luma sees his eyes turn gold and hears the stones go quiet, afraid. The Sunbolt and
  the Third Eye grow stronger, and every crew in Las Veyra pays tribute (gang heat is cleared).

Either way the world stays open after the credits: the Third Eye can still step into the Veil, creatures still roam
both sides, and the guilds, contracts, bosses and Free Roam carry on.
