# Visual Rebirth 04A art bible

The large forms are inhabited stone terraces, supporting arch ribs, layered roof crowns and branching mineral gardens. Deep portals and structural support precede small ornament. Hero content uses the same source kit as the distant city. All new artwork is original procedural geometry and authored maps generated by the scripts in `tools/`; no external assets or reference images were used.

| Region | Structure and silhouette | Material and plant language | Light |
|---|---|---|---|
| Amethyst Covenant | Pale arcades, narrow layered spires, observatory instruments | Limestone, blue-black roofs, silver/copper trim, indigo woven banners | Cool mineral cores and warm lanterns, quiet stone |
| Dawnstone Court | Broad terraces, stepped roof shoulders, open sun courts | Warm limestone, patinated copper, dry gold flowers | Amber accents against cool shade |
| Verdant Shard | Root arches, branching trees, aerial foliage over masonry | Moss-green stone, split bark, individual leaf blades, ferns and shelf fungi | Aquamarine crystal seams and low warm path lights |
| Obsidian Rift | Fractured battlements, extraction engines, asymmetric mineral masses | Dark weathered basalt, scorched metal, restrained red cloth | Amber/red fractures, readable unlit stone |

Doors, gates and paths use human scale: 3m minimum elevated route width and a 9m open cross in the surrounding courts. The six construction sockets keep their original functional positions. New neighborhood plots use the same 48m route/POI/terrain survey as the original forts; content is skipped when the survey fails. Ornamental roofs are not marked as landing platforms. New intended walking surfaces have separately authored matching collision.

The surface library contains 11 families with albedo, tangent normal, packed metallic/occlusion/smoothness, and emission maps. Roughness comes from material response, not excessive bloom. Limestone smoothness is about .25, bark .17, cloth .18, copper .48, crystal .78 and water .85. Stained glass is opaque patterned glass for bounded sorting and shader compatibility. Crystal reflection/refraction is an opaque faceted illusion. There is no expensive screen-space transmission or new render pipeline.

Leaf canopies use actual tapered blades on branches, with separate trunk and foliage masters. Leaves remain opaque; silhouette variation comes from geometry rather than transparent balloon cards. Individual ferns, reeds, flowers, roots, fungi and moss-like ground palettes provide the smaller scale. Several canopy species, regional choices and five seeded building variants reduce repetition. The same authored outlines remain at Low with cheaper geometry.

Texture masks are R metallic, G ambient occlusion, B reserved and A smoothness. Normal and mask maps import in linear space. Color/emission maps import in sRGB. Every surface has a retained `.mat` for Built-in inspection; the runtime constructs the corresponding native Lit material for the selected Built-in/URP mode from the same manifest. Styles and source meshes are shared across the world and reset with the existing game lifecycle.

Lighting changes are restrained and weighted out in the Veil and Frostline. One shared scheduler caps animated rings/vines, pollen fields, accent lights and cosmetic drake roosts. No new constant hostile spawn is introduced. Existing interactive habitat, discovery and fort capture systems retain their own behavior.

The offline review rig renders shipping geometry and shipping layout data. It is useful for composition and asset defects; it cannot establish Unity shadow behavior, game-camera readability, traversal, frame rate or final in-game appearance. The included Unity QA capture command records the five time/weather looks on the owner's machine.
