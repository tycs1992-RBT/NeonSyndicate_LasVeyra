# Cheat Codes

Type a code on the keyboard while playing (no menu needed). A notice confirms it worked.

None of the codes contain the letters M or P, because those keys open the map and the phone.
Codes that turn something on also turn it off when typed again.

## Debug menu

Press **F2** during play for a debug menu with buttons for most of the above, plus:

- teleport to any shop, safehouse, contact or landmark
- spawn any of the 34 vehicles (the 30 hover cars and the 4 hover bikes)
- set the wanted level from 0 to 5 stars
- change time and weather
- start any story mission directly
- the Progress tab: skill points, a free skill reset, Lumen, materials, every Forge recipe, cash, open any Forge, and
  raise or lower any faction's reputation (see Docs/PROGRESSION.md)
- the Creatures, Quests and Events tabs: spawn creatures, fight a boss in its arena, start any quest or world event
  (see Docs/CREATURES.md and Docs/QUESTS_AND_EVENTS.md)
- the Loot tab: a gun of each rarity, one of each gun type, every unique gun, weapon cores, a champion enemy next to
  you, and the pity meter (see Docs/LOOT.md)
- the Travel tab: unlock every travel power, go back to story unlocks, show the button hints again (see Docs/TRAVEL.md)
- the Gear tab (Phase 6): unlock every gadget, reset gadget cooldowns, show the gadget hints again, drop a gadget
  mod of each rarity, wear four Legendary pieces of any build set, force a city tier (1 to 5, not saved), spawn three
  hostile targets to test gadgets on, and read the gear score breakdown and the active set bonuses (see Docs/GADGETS.md)
- the Loot world tab: reveal every chest on the map, 5 Chest Keys, respawn every chest, go to the nearest chest or
  hidden satchel, mark 10 satchels found, post a new bounty board, drop a Chest Key, call the Collector (see
  Docs/LOOTWORLD.md)
- the Cover tab: show every cover spot around you, switch enemy cover tactics on or off, spawn a squad of each enemy
  role (Riflemen, Rushers, Heavies, Marksmen, Engineers, or a mixed squad), live squad numbers (see Docs/COVER.md)

Your bike is always one phone call away: Phone > Bring my bike (see Docs/VEHICLES.md).

Cheats don't block saving or achievements.
