# Loot guns

Randomized guns in the looter-shooter style: every gun is rolled from a type, a maker, seven parts, a rarity, an item
level, maybe an element and a few traits. Enemies drop them in beams of light, tougher enemies drop better ones, and
weapon cores let you rebuild them at the Forge. Nothing here has been run in Unity yet: the code compiles in all twelve
configurations and the maths is tested offline, but how it looks and feels needs your eyes (checklist at the end).

## How it fits the game

- **Four loot slots.** Loot guns you equip sit in four loot slots that behave like any other weapon: the number keys,
  the weapon wheel (their names show in their rarity colour), D-pad left and right, drive-bys for one-handed guns,
  first person, saves. Your shop and story weapons stay exactly as they were.
- **The backpack** holds 30 more guns. See them all in the pause menu's **ARSENAL** tab.
- **Ammo** is shared with the normal guns of the same kind (a loot SMG uses SMG rounds). A gun you pick up comes with
  two magazines. Crystal casters need no ammo at all.

## Rarities

| Rarity | Colour | Damage | Traits | Beam |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Common | grey | x0.85 | none | short |
| Uncommon | green | x1.0 (the shop guns' level) | sometimes 1 | knee high |
| Rare | blue | x1.1 | 1 | head high |
| Epic | purple | x1.2 | 2 | tall, with a light and a minimap mark |
| Legendary | orange | x1.32 | 2 signature traits | 14 m tower, outer glow, ground ring, fanfare |
| Mythic | cyan | x1.45 | 3 signature traits | 22 m tower, outer glow, ground ring, fanfare |

Every Legendary and Mythic drop is a **named unique** with a flavor line (17 of them, five Mythics themed on the four
creature bosses plus one more). **Item level** (1 to 50) comes from your player level, plus 1 for elites, 2 for
champions and great beasts, 3 for bosses; each level adds 0.8% damage.

The numbers are capped on purpose: no gun of any rarity or level can take down even the weakest Shard-Touched elite
(352 health) with one body shot, so a Legendary feels strong without trivialising fights on Veteran. Headshots and
critical hits can still do it.

## The eight types

Pistol, Hand Cannon, SMG, Assault Rifle, Shotgun, Sniper Rifle, Launcher (a grenade launcher, or a rocket launcher when
its body part rolls the heavy frames) and the **Crystal Caster**: a crystal-tech energy gun with no magazine. It heats up
as you fire (a bar under the crosshair) and vents when it overheats; press reload to vent early.

## The six makers

| Maker | Personality | Look |
|---|---|---|
| Saltglass Optics | precise: tight spread, big criticals, a bit slower | grown pale ceramic, bone ribs, tuning-fork emitter |
| Brassjaw Foundry | heavy hitting: big damage, slow, kicks hard | beetle-wing brass plates, mandible jaw |
| Veyra Crystalworks | elemental: always has an element, and it hits harder | floating crystal shards, prism core, crystal lance |
| Cinderline Rapid Arms | fast firing: very high fire rate, lighter rounds | segmented black chitin, red seams, triple spines |
| Dunehold Munitions | high capacity: huge magazines, slower reloads | sandstone nautilus shell, egg drum on top |
| Oddwire Labs | gimmick: one extra trait on every gun | mismatched coils, tesla rings, hovering orb sight |

Names read prefix + maker + model, for example "Searing Brassjaw Gavel": the prefix comes from the element (Searing,
Glacial...) or from the gun's most notable part (Long, Steady, Bottomless...).

## Parts

Body, barrel, grip, stock, sight, magazine and accessory, four variants each (variant 0 is plain). Each changes stats
and the model: a long barrel adds range, a drum magazine adds rounds but slows reloads, a long scope tightens aim and
adds a zoom, an element coil raises the element chance, and so on. Their combined effect is clamped so no mix runs away.

## Looks

None of the guns are human firearms. There are no boxy receivers, rails, hanging magazines or wooden stocks: every
maker is an alien design language, built from lofted shells, shards, fins, rings and plates (all generated by
`LootGunModels`, flat shaded, five submeshes: body, metal, accent, rarity glow, element glow).

| Maker | Body | Emitter (the barrel's place) | Ammo (the magazine's place) | Stock |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Saltglass | a grown pale ceramic shell with a ridge, bone ribs wrapped unevenly over it, a tall fin on the left and a small one on the right | a split tuning fork: a short stem and two tines, the element sparks between them | a seed pod set into the underside at a forward slant | a grown tail, a tail with a keel, two bone struts, or a flared pad |
| Brassjaw | a dark soft core under overlapping brass wing plates, split down the spine, widening to the rear, rear edges lifted | a segmented throat ending in hooked mandibles that close around the muzzle, a glowing tongue inside | a gizzard sac slanting back under the body | a drooping tail of shrinking plates, or two brass horns to a pad |
| Veyra | crystal shards floating around a glowing prism core with gaps everywhere, glowing nodes in the gaps | no tube: a crystal lance tapering to a point, ringed by orbiting splinters | a floating crystal cluster under the core | a trail of shrinking shards, a V of thin shards, or a big butt crystal |
| Cinderline | black chitin in segments like an insect abdomen, glowing red seams between them, exhaust gills flaring from the rear flanks | three thin parallel spines rooted in a collar | a fuel capsule half sunk into the left flank (a ring tank on the drum variant) | a segmented tail, or one curved spine to a chitin shield |
| Dunehold | a sandstone nautilus coil riding on top at the rear, its last whorl running forward as the body | a conch horn that flares to a lip | a drum of eggs turning on an axle along the top | a tail that curls into a small coil |
| Oddwire | a core wrapped in copper coils of different sizes, off centre and tilted, a capacitor bulb on one side, loose wires as tubes | a bare rod with a ball tip, ringed by tesla rings that shrink toward the muzzle | a battery canister on the flank with a wire into the body | a rod with a pad, a fat coil, or a helix spring |

Every part variant changes the model: **body** (1 lightweight and open, 2 reinforced with extra plates or bosses, 3
tuned and raked), **barrel** (1 long, 2 serrated or barbed or ridged, 3 heavy with a collar), **grip** (a horn-bent
handle: plain, thick with finger ridges, slim with a spur, or with a thumb knob), **stock**, **sight** (bead spikes, a
seed eye, an arched loop with a lens, an eye-stalk scope; Oddwire always uses a hovering orb), **magazine** (the ammo
organ's size and count) and **accessory** (a lamp pod, a crown of spikes at the muzzle, or three glowing halos).
Rarity adds glowing **veins** in its colour that follow each maker's shapes (one, two or three per side), named uniques
grow rarity-coloured crystal shards, and the **element** lights the core (Saltglass's spine filament and fork spark,
Brassjaw's tongue, Veyra's prism, Cinderline's eye slit, Dunehold's eggs and coil eye, Oddwire's rod and bulb).

Kept from the first pass, so holds and holsters still fit: the grip origin, +Z barrel, the Muzzle and Foregrip children,
the submesh order, and the overall length per archetype (within 15 percent on average; the offline harness measures
it). Triangle counts stay under twice the first pass for every gun (153k versus 130k over all 384 test configurations,
the largest 828). Previews rendered offline (flat colours, no lighting model of Unity) are in `Docs/Phase5_Previews`.
In Unity, check: do the six makers read as alien at third-person distance, are the rarity veins visible, does the
support hand sit on the lobe under the emitter, and does the muzzle flash come out of the fork, jaw, lance tip, spines,
horn and ball tip.

## Elements

| Element | What it does on a hit (chance shown on the card) |
|---|---|
| Volt | lightning arcs to the nearest other enemy |
| Ember | sets the target on fire (the normal burning) |
| Frost | drains poise, so enemies and creatures stagger |
| Void | heals you a little |

Elements also count against creature weaknesses and resistances (a frost gun hits fire creatures harder and frost
creatures softer), and bullets still find creature weak points. Volt rounds also do 50% more damage to enemy armour
plates, and every element shows its colour on the floating damage numbers.

**Guns and powers feed each other (Phase 6).** Loot guns charge the crystal bar like every gun, detonate the marks
crystal powers leave (a Resonance Burst refills the magazine, or vents a crystal caster), strip enemy armour plates and
break creature parts. See [COMBAT_LOOP.md](COMBAT_LOOP.md).

## Traits

Ricochet, Piercing, Explosive Rounds, Chain Lightning, Micro Rockets (every third shot, homing), Split Shot, Burst Fire,
Charge Shot (hold to charge, release to fire up to 80% harder; tapping fires normally), Double Barrel, Refill on Kill,
Life Steal, Critical Cascade, Empty Nova (when the magazine empties, or a caster overheats), Penetrator (through thin
cover) and Spin Up (fire rate climbs while you hold the trigger). Each type only rolls traits that make sense for it.
Explosive-round splash, lightning arcs, micro rockets and novas only ever hit enemies, never bystanders (a launcher's own blast hurts anyone close, as launchers always have).

## Who drops what

| Enemy | Gun drop chance | Rarity | Weapon cores |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gang, police, SWAT, hostiles | 6 to 14% | mostly Common and Uncommon | none |
| Shard-Touched elites | always | Rare or better | 35% one elemental core |
| **Champions** | always, plus 50% a second gun | Epic or better | always one, sometimes a Prism Core |
| Wild creatures | 2% | mostly Common and Uncommon | none |
| Elite creatures | 25% | Rare or better | 25% one core |
| Great beasts (Dune Wyrm, Quartz Drake) | 60% | Epic or better | often |
| Creature bosses | always | Legendary, 15% Mythic (its own themed unique) | two elemental, one Prism, sometimes an Apex Core |

**Veteran** drops guns 25% more often and favours Epic, Legendary and Mythic. **Pity:** after 19 drops in a row
below Epic, the 20th is an Epic. At most 32 drops lie in the world (the oldest, lowest rarity goes first); Common and
Uncommon fade after 2 minutes, Rare after 3, Epic and up after 5 (they blink for the last 10 seconds).

**City tier (Phase 6).** Your gear score sets a city tier from 1 to 5. Each tier above 1 adds item levels to gun drops
(+1, +2, +4, +6, fed into the generator's level input by `GearScoreRules.LootItemLevel`) and gives every rolled gun a
6 / 12 / 18 / 25% chance to come one rarity higher (never up to Mythic), applied before the pity count. Gadget mods
also drop from kills. See `Docs/GADGETS.md` and the BUILDS section of `Docs/PROGRESSION.md`.

## Champions

Named enemies (for example "Vex the Unbroken") with one to three affixes: **Armored** (30% less damage, heavy armour),
**Shielded** (a shield that refills the armour plates still on when left alone), **Swift**, **Volatile** (explodes a moment after dying: back
off), **Vampiric** (heals when it hurts you) and **Regenerating**. They glow purple, carry a name plate with their
affixes and a health bar, show on the minimap and have 5.5 times the health of a normal enemy. Now and then a gang
member out in gang territory becomes one, and in a fight against three or more enemies one of them may step up as a
lieutenant. Never during missions, at most two at a time.

## Weapon cores and the Forge

Six new materials: **Volt, Ember, Frost and Void Cores** (elemental), the **Prism Core** and the rare **Apex Core**.
At any Forge except a shrine, the **ARMORY** tab lists your loot guns and shows each change as NOW and AFTER side by
side before you pay, so no stat ever drops by surprise:

- **Infuse**: give a gun an element, or change it, with one core of that element (two for Legendary and up).
- **Ascend**: one rarity step up. One part is rerolled (the screen says which, and into what), every trait is kept,
  and a new trait is added when the new rarity calls for more. Costs rise with rarity: Prism Cores, then Apex Cores,
  and a boss heart for Mythic.
- **Raise level**: bring a gun up to your level (cash and shard chunks, Prism Cores for Epic and up).
- **Scrap**: break a gun into materials and cores (rarer guns give Prism and Apex Cores back).

The Forgewrights' fee discount applies to the cash.

## The ARSENAL tab and the gun shop

Pause menu > ARSENAL: the four loot slots and the backpack grid in rarity frames. Pick a gun to see its card (stats with
green and red differences against the gun it would replace), then EQUIP 1 to 4, UNEQUIP, FAVORITE (favorites cannot be
scrapped or sold), COMPARE (cycles which slot it compares with) and SCRAP (press twice). Mouse, arrow keys or the
controller all work.

**Selling happens only at gun shops**: Gun shop > Loot guns lists your backpack with sell prices, and a rotating stock of
four Common to Rare loot guns at four times their sell value (new stock every 15 minutes of play).

## Debug page

F2 > Loot: spawn a gun of each rarity in front of you, one Rare of each of the eight types, every unique in a line, give
5 of every core, spawn a champion next to you, fill the pity meter, clear dropped guns. The top line shows your level,
backpack, live drops, pity count and live champions.

## What to test in Unity

1. F2 > Loot > "Every unique": walk the line. Do the beams read clearly by rarity (Legendary and Mythic should be
   unmistakable from down the street)? Do the six makers look different? Are the guns the right size in the hand, is the
   support hand on the foregrip for rifles, shotguns, snipers, launchers and casters (and the old rifles, LMG, sniper,
   launchers and minigun)?
2. Stand at a beam: does the pickup card appear, with the right button (E, or Y / Triangle on a controller)? Tap to take,
   hold to equip. Check the gun appears in the weapon wheel in its rarity colour and in first person.
3. Fire every type with a controller and with the mouse: burst, charge shot, spin up, the caster's heat bar and vent,
   micro rockets, ricochets off walls, explosive rounds, chain lightning, the empty nova.
4. Spawn a champion: name plate, aura, affixes, the drop when it dies (Epic or better plus a core banner).
5. Pause > ARSENAL with the controller only: move through slots and backpack, equip, favorite, scrap.
6. Forge > ARMORY: infuse, ascend, raise level, scrap; check NOW and AFTER match what you get.
7. Save, quit, load: the same guns in the same slots, with the same names and numbers.
8. Gun shop > Loot guns: buy and sell.

## The loot world (Phase 5)

Guns also come out of the world itself: 140 chests across the city, the Badlands and the Frostline (common pods, sealed
chests that need a Chest Key and are guarded by a crew that scales with your level, and Veil Vaults inside the Inner
World with Epic or better guns), 40 hidden satchels on rooftops, tower ledges and under the canal bridges, and the
Collector, a phone contact who pays a guaranteed Rare, Epic and Legendary gun at 10, 25 and 40 satchels. Chest guns are
normal drops (they count toward pity). Gun shops sell Chest Keys in the Loot guns tab, the ARSENAL tab shows the tally,
and quests can now reward a loot gun with the GiveGun step. Everything is in `Docs/LOOTWORLD.md`.
