## What is actually different

Real adult anatomical face, nose/ears/lips, textured skin with normal detail, independent sclera/iris/pupil/cornea, original short scalp and facial grooms, actual articulated fingers, layered split coat with thickness, leather underlayer/trousers, curved armor lames/chest pieces, bronze setting and restrained amethyst seal. No extra procedural fallback polygons were added. Real near/mid/far geometry and explicit visual sockets were exported.

Development views exposed opaque eye veiling, coarse shiny groom response and exposed shoulder/elbow gaps. The source cornea was replaced with a transparent highlight mixture, the groom response corrected, and the fitted underlayer extended across anatomical shoulders and arms. Side/rear/close and stress views are deliberately supplied so remaining flaws are not hidden by framing.
