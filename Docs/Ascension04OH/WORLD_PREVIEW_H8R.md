# Limited H8R world-art foundation

Actual editable library: ArtSource/WorldFoundationH8R/WorldFoundationH8R.blend. Six real UV-mapped FBXs are under Assets/NeonSyndicate/Art/WorldFoundationH8R: SanctuaryPortal, ProcessionalVault, SanctuaryStair, RootSpringArch, ShardwoodElder and RootMineralBank. They are original project meshes, CC0-1.0.

The portal has coursed support mass, radial voussoirs, stepped buttresses and mineral footings. The vault repeats structural ribs. The stair has actual tread/riser geometry. The cave mouth is an open irregular stone envelope. The tree has a branching trunk/root system and leaf sprays. The mineral bank contains rooted ground shapes and cyan growths. This is a small foundation, not a completed architecture/forest/cave overhaul.

The explicit Editor overlay places portal/gallery, tree/Azure, mineral bank/shrine and cave arch/cavern near original anchors in Gothic Civic H5. It preserves existing collision. New stairs/floors are deliberately excluded from revision 10. Do not infer walkability, navmesh, companion/vehicle clearance, camera safety or performance from asset bounds or source renders. These require native acceptance. Production colliders, richer materials, full terrain/cave systems and broader placement remain unfinished.
