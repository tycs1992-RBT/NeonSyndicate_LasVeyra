## Character deliverables

Malik is an adult protective father with medium-brown skin, believable adult anatomy, expressive eyes, credible skull/eye sockets/lids/nose/lips/ears/jaw/neck/hands, a short undercut and neat short beard. Original character identity, not a copy of any reference-media actor.

Create a sculpted and retopologized head with eye/iris/cornea geometry, credible skin and roughness, safe hair/beard alpha cards or equivalent, clean transitions and neutral/emotion samples. Deliver actual facial shapes/rig if expression performance is included; concept illustrations are not geometry or facial animation.

Wardrobe: long split weathered charcoal traveling coat, flexible leather underlayers, dark articulated metal protection, aged bronze fasteners, useful belts, boots, restrained amethyst fittings and Third Eye accent. The silhouette must read from normal third-person, 5m/20m and dialogue camera. Actual cloth folds, seams and thickness; shoulder/coat-tail weights must survive guard, aim, crouch, dodge, attack and flight. Avoid full-body fluorescent plating. Separate skin, eyes, hair, cloth, leather and metal material response.

Provide FBX with applied positive uniform transforms, grounded pivot, declared units/up/forward conventions, a clean skinned skeleton and normalized complete weights. Prefer a native-tested four-weight production export compatible with existing presets; do not use the rejected H5 pruning recipe as a final export. All original 47 gameplay semantics and fingers must map through RigLayout.Names/ImportedBindH3, or a reviewed adapter. Received reference skeleton has 65 bones and all bindings preserved, but does not set the artistic skeleton budget.

Deliver matching near/mid/far LOD meshes with actual counts and bounds, texture/material inventory and no hidden duplicate body geometry. Supply skin/cloth/leather/metal albedo, normal, roughness, metallic/AO and appropriate hair alpha. Include texture color-space/normal convention; shader assignments must work in the existing Built-in path, with optional URP checked separately. Agree actual triangle/material/texture targets after declared-hardware profiling rather than promising FPS.

Visual locators: hand_r, hand_l, foregrip, muzzle, blade_hilt, blade_tip, head, chest, fx, synergy, plus appropriate sheath attachments. Original gameplay sockets, IK, weapon sweeps, inventory and damage remain authoritative. No controllers, gameplay scripts, colliders, extra health, camera or root-motion ownership in the supplied prefab.
