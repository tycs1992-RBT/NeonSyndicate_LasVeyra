# H5 independent skin proof

Original H3 FBXs and failed reports remain immutable. Raw body/clothing sum errors remain .915488897 and 1.09097932. This is separate from the unchanged engine-dependent NUnit failures.

Actual binary FBX inspection confirms linear skin data with finite nonnegative influences, nonzero total for every skinned vertex and preserved cluster/bone/bind/geometry properties. The source cluster uses the normalizing/default convention. The parser's raw weights are not guaranteed normalized. A separate derivative divides every positive influence by its vertex total, retaining all source influences (at most 6 body, 7 clothing). Zero contributions are omitted. No geometry, normals, UVs, bind matrices, IDs, units or skeleton transforms are changed. All five original binary files also round-trip byte-identically through the writer without a requested edit; footer alignment/mandatory zeros are preserved.

For a vertex with source influences w_i and transformed positions T_i p, the comparison is sum(w_i T_i p)/sum(w_i). The full derivative stores w_i/sum(w_i), giving the same normalized linear result. Independent C/ufbx inspection checks the real original/derived FBXs, bone names/binds and eight deterministic joint-stress pose matrices, over 180968 vertex/pose samples across the five files. Maximum full-derivative differences are about 6.67e-16m body and 8.89e-16m clothing. Strict sum errors are at most 2.23e-16. These are source math/FBX results, not a Unity import or realistic movement acceptance.

The alternative top-four rule ranks positive weights descending with source cluster ID as deterministic tie break, keeps four, then renormalizes. It discards up to 8.3934 percent of normalized mass. Under the same unchanged 5mm source deformation bound, body error 5.505116mm and clothing 19.503309mm FAIL. That four-weight export is not selected or shipped as the H5 reference. Failed reports remain in EvidenceH5. This is not hidden by a normalization-only pass.
