# Owner walkthrough: preserve progress and identify the actual failure

This is a short procedure to collect evidence and try the repaired preparation. It is not a completed playtest. Do not create a new game or overwrite your only save to reproduce a screenshot.

1. **Before changing the current session**, press F2 and open **Fantasy proof**. Capture the visible branch, commit, player position and the FR01 and FR02A rows, including `prepared`, `selected`, `observed active` and first refusal. Also capture the WORLD REWRITE rows. If possible, use the scroll wheel to include the complete reasons. If those rows are blank or a different version, record that too.
2. Open Unity's **Window > General > Console**. Select the `Physics.ClosestPoint` warning and copy its full text and stack trace, plus the action just performed and whether it repeats. A status-bar line alone does not identify a cause. Record the first red import/compile error separately. Don't clear the Console before saving the evidence.
3. Save your progress in the normal way and back up your existing saves. In GitHub Desktop, select `feature/04oh-ascension-art-foundation`, **Fetch origin** and **Pull origin**; preserve unrelated local changes. The update requires Unity to recompile. Stop Play only after preserving the current progress; wait for import and compilation. Use Unity 6000.3.16f1 and the existing project.
4. In the menu select **Neon Syndicate > Acceptance > Verify Fantasy World**. In that window click **1. Verify preparation and source identity**. This button performs preparation; opening the window alone does not. Check the displayed prepared commit and all blockers. If blocked, save the exact first blocker and the Console errors. Do not assume a newly prepared Archive resolves a separate FR01/FR02A failure.
5. Resume an existing safe Free Roam session without starting a new game. From Pause > **EXPLORE**, choose **PLAY FANTASY WORLD** if the ordinary session permits it. Press F2 > **Fantasy proof** again and photograph the same FR01/FR02A and World Rewrite rows. A prepared or active label does not establish visual quality.
6. Walk from the Lofts/home court toward the Archive and Forge. Confirm which doors actually open and whether the Archive lectern, Dante and the existing anvil are reachable. Look for a believable street, useful home and work spaces, and any misleading empty facade. Use **Route: Stone threshold**, **Route: Mineral grotto**, then **Route: Woodland causeway** in EXPLORE and follow the existing GPS; an active mission can take priority. Return normally. Use the original JOURNAL and quest prerequisites; do not force rewards or progression.
7. At each of the two reported structures, capture a ground-level view and a normal-camera view, then, only if your existing game has earned flight, a short ordinary flight alongside and into the reported wall and over the roof. Record whether contact stopped the player or a roof allowed landing. Give each structure a recognizable landmark and, if convenient, its position from F2. Do not use teleporting to establish reachability.
8. If the load fails again, a wall is fly-through, the roof is unsupported, a door or route is blocked, or the world barely changes, mark that check FAIL and keep its evidence. Exit the temporary preview or use the existing OFF/compare control to return to its original presentation; the exact before/after state must still be observed. Do not accept the art based on a label.

The existing Verify window's native-review button has a save-backup option and can start a fresh Free Roam review. Use it only if you deliberately want that separate review after protecting your saves; it is not needed to preserve or investigate the current session. See `INVESTIGATION.md` and `COLLISION_AND_PURPOSE.md` for what is and is not known.
