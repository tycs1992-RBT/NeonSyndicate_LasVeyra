# Existing first-region circuit

The intended route starts at home, visits Dante and the Archive, reaches the threshold and mineral grotto, continues to Azure Glade and returns to the Forge. The existing EXPLORE page provides individual waypoints. Quest progress remains controlled by the original JOURNAL and the original giver; route buttons do not begin or complete a quest.

Stone, Not Silence precedes Where the Trees Ring in the original data. Availability still depends on the existing quest and world rules. An active mission target takes precedence over an EXPLORE waypoint; a waypoint takes precedence over a tracked campaign lead until it is reached or cleared. Existing Underheart and Pressure House routes remain separate options.

The destinations are source-wired to the original owners. Walking continuity, travel time, NPC interaction, any encounter, quest completion and a safe return have not been observed in native Unity.
