## Forge-area refusal

The playtest reports a line 7 placement `near Venue:forge.exit` refused at the protected `Under:canals.transit` quest place for `uh05_06_pressure`, including the camera margin. Two checked-in placement files have this same line number and anchor: `Art/LiveWorld/SanctuaryPortal/placement.txt` requests offset `-21 0 -12`, and `Art/LiveWorld/ShardwoodElder/placement.txt` requests `-45 0 -16`. The old on-screen message omitted the art name; the screenshot cannot distinguish the two and may reflect a local placement file. It is incorrect to assert which one was refused without its F2/Console entry.

The quest really belongs to the underground transit tunnel. The source has the anchor at y = -26, with an encounter radius of 16 m; the placement guard includes a 2 m quest margin. The original placement guard projected every quest radius onto X/Z and therefore treated it as extending indefinitely upward. This can reject a surface candidate even when the full candidate and quest are vertically separated. That defect is independent of the Fantasy World catalog error.

The new source treats an explicitly resolved `Under:` quest as a protected volume. The volume extends at least the quest radius plus the camera and a further 4 m vertically; a piece that overlaps it still keeps the previous plan-radius and camera-margin rules. Surface quests, ordinary interactions, venue doors, realm and walk routes, blocked openings, physical overlap and ground probes retain their existing checks. Invalid infinite-radius data uses the previous planar protection. The on-screen refusal now includes the piece name, preserving the actual failure detail if it remains blocked. The new rule does **not** declare the placement safe: another guard, the actual candidate bounds, or Unity physics can still refuse it. The exact final position and outcome were not tested in Unity.

## Connected outdoor change

The Heart presentation now stages a continuous stone-and-carved-edge street across the existing `home-court`, `court-crossing`, `sanctuary-approach` and `forge-market` route rectangles. The planner clips the centre and edging around junctions instead of placing overlapping rectangular street tiles, and refuses missing, inconsistent or malformed authored routes. It uses the already prepared FR01 masonry and carved-trim materials, with their existing required texture-map validation. It generates no new collider: the existing supported realm routes and their original collision, services, actors and quests retain authority. The previous FR01 paving is staged as a fallback, and is restored if the candidate fails while that fallback is valid. If both are invalid, neither is displayed and the underlying original supported road remains; the refusal is logged. If preparation or layout validation fails before staging, the original route code is used.

This is actual runtime source integration, but it is a limited ground-level change. It does not change the silhouette of the large structure, produce a complete Gothic district, or demonstrate that the Fantasy preview can currently load. Nothing in the new change proves that the street is attractive, visible in the current session, flush at all junctions, walkable or free of camera issues. Existing exterior material work from the prior checkpoint is unchanged.
