# The Shattered Metropolis, implemented world direction

Las Veyra remains the settlement shaped by the broken Heart of Crystal Manor. Malik and Luma, the original twelve chapters, the Shard Warden, both First Laws, six earned weapon focuses, BONDS identities and every prior side story retain their original owners and saved identities. The newer documentation Bible in PR9 was read as compatible creative direction, not merged or used to override canon.

The revised preview applies clustered composition to the existing city. Service hubs retain their original hosts; intervening ordinary parcels become low shattered fringe. Six inhabited grammars use civic mineral halls, stepped hearth residences, asymmetrical forge vaults, raised canal compounds, split signal ruins and garden towers. Collectible roofs keep their original height and footprint on actual piers rather than full-height curtain walls. This is an initial production-art implementation, not visual acceptance.

Four Spine segments form a visible mineral system: the Heart's Broken Rib near home, the Working Seam in Old Silver, the Listening Rupture in Grave Frequency and the Root Below near the Pressure House/Underheart approach. Street-level mineral inlays follow retained real road routes between segments. They grant no powers, loot or fast travel. Crowns are collidable scenery with near/far variants; decorative inlays have no collision or attack logic.

Ordinary residents, shops, guilds, Forge, wildlife, territory, encounters and quests continue through their original directors. The first original Discovery puzzle is searched near home in this profile, retaining its existing reward receipt. The Working Seam is a finite, optional four-creature Muster with the established Cantor. No wildlife is made bondable and no companion is granted.

The city has not yet achieved its final compressed road topology. The 562 rebuilt lots and33 courtyard passages are actual source changes, but main service journey lengths are unchanged. The legacy world is still the default until native route, story and save acceptance permits promotion.
