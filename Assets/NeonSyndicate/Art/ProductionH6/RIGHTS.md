# H6 candidate source rights and exclusions

All final graphical sources are CC0-1.0, or original project garment/groom/texture authoring released here as CC0-1.0. Source code of external authoring tools is not copied into this project; its separate software licenses are not asserted to be CC0.

Anatomical mesh, morph targets and standard rig: MakeHuman Community / MPFB core graphics. Core author declarations identify Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius and Jonas Hauquier. Exact MPFB source commit b1a2dc98776da9d1eea751b159d42cf61778fe2e. Core graphics license: https://static.makehumancommunity.org/about/license.html . Body geometry contains an explicit September 2020 CC0 declaration. Actual source is https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2 .

Skin albedo, normal and source specular: Mindfront, natural male skin source, explicitly listed CC0 at https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/skins02.html . Original selected archive https://files.makehumancommunity.org/asset_packs/skins02/skins02_cc0.zip . Actual original PNG hashes and conversions are in Textures/texture-lineage.json. The soft scalp-root overlay and fabric microstructure are original project authoring. The original JPEG/source specular are retained inputs, not additional active materials.

Eye geometry reference, eye image, brows/lashes and boots: official MakeHuman core assets, CC0. The eye texture came from official core commit a8bc2d54ff0ac92e78ff71431b1023eda42bf482 at makehuman/data/eyes/materials/brown_eye.png . Other actual selected core parts came from https://files2.makehumancommunity.org/asset_packs/makehuman_system_assets/makehuman_system_assets_cc0.zip . This was a selected-member download with CRC checks, not a claim that the entire large archive was acquired.

The initial WDG beard trial had conflicting legacy AGPL file-header and pack CC0 labeling. It is excluded. No trial mask, mesh or texture is used in the final candidate, and no blanket relicensing was asserted. The final facial groom is independently authored directly from the CC0 anatomical face surface. No purchased or restricted vendor model, commercial-media character, concept image as geometry, private rights receipt or player save is included.
