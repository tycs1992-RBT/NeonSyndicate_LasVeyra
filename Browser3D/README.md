# Neon Syndicate: Las Veyra (3D browser version)

A full 3D open-world game that runs in Chrome. Third-person cameras on foot (the default frames your character in the middle about four meters back, plus a tight over-the-shoulder view and a far view), first person, a chase camera and a driver's-eye view in cars, sunny palm-lined streets with power lines, weeds along the chain-link and cracked sidewalks, traffic that stops at lights, pedestrians and joggers, carjacking, guns, a five-star wanted level with police cars, officers on foot and a helicopter, three story missions, day and night, radio and HUD.

## New direction, phase 1: a city with two faces

This build is the first playable look at the new direction for the game. The Unity version picked up the hover cars and heat crystals in phase 2; the Inner World and the Frostline are browser-only for now.

- **The Outer City:** the same streets, now a future city. Every car is a hover car riding on glowing thruster pads with underglow. Buildings carry neon trim (colour by district), palms and trees glow faintly, street light is cool white-blue, and the billboards advertise enhancement clinics and shard grafts. About half the people you pass are enhanced, with glowing visors, eye implants, collars or temple lines (you have one too).
- **The Inner World:** press **T** to open your Third Eye. The image inverts for an instant, then the sky turns violet over a blood-red horizon, a rift opens in the sky, crystal growths erupt from the buildings and sidewalks, spores drift, the streets empty, and the **Hollows** (tall dark figures with glowing eyes and crystal crowns) come for you. Press **T** again to come back. Nobody reports gunfire in there.
- **The Frostline:** drive north out of the city through the FROSTLINE gate. Snowfields with pines, boulders, ice formations and a frozen lake run up to snow-capped mountains, snow falls, the wind picks up, and **Frost Stalkers** hunt anything that wanders in. A research outpost and radio mast sit at the end of the road.
- Heat now shows as crystal shards instead of stars, and the sidewalks are properly busy again (people used to trickle in a few at a time).

## Phase 2 balance

Fights match the Unity build's Balanced difficulty: hits on you land about 40 percent softer, your health creeps back up to 60 percent after five seconds without taking damage, and the camera pulls back about a metre (with a slightly wider view) while a fight is on, so you can see who is coming. The big Phase 2 features (sculpted characters, IK animation, dodge, parry, gear and controller support) are in the Unity version.

Coming in later phases (design agreed, not built yet): skill trees (Body, Mind, Shard), companions and guilds, gear and loot from creatures, more zones with recommended levels (the Badlands), and online co-op for up to four players, with PvP later.

## Play

1. Double-click **NeonSyndicate3D.html**. Chrome is recommended (Edge and Safari also work).
2. Wait for "Ready", then click **Play**. Your mouse is captured; press **Esc** to get it back and pause.
3. Walk up to the white convertible beside you and press **F**.

Nothing to install and no internet needed. The whole game (city, people, cars, guns, sounds, music) is generated by code inside that one file.

## Controls

| Key | On foot | In a car |
|---|---|---|
| W A S D | Move | Gas, brake/reverse, steer |
| Mouse | Look and aim | Look around (recenters by itself) |
| Right mouse / Left mouse | Aim / Shoot or punch | Drive-by (pistol or SMG) |
| Shift / Alt / Ctrl / Space | Sprint / Walk / Crouch / Jump | Space = handbrake |
| R | Reload | |
| Mouse wheel, 1 to 5, hold Tab | Change weapon, weapon wheel | Change drive-by weapon |
| F | Get in or steal a car | Get out (at speed you dive out) |
| V | Camera: standard, far, first person, close shoulder | Camera: chase, far, driver's eyes |
| T | Third Eye: enter or leave the Inner World | Same |
| E | Start a mission, shop, save | |
| H / Q / C | | Horn (siren in a cruiser) / Radio station / Look behind |
| M / Esc or P | Map (click to set a waypoint) / Pause, controls, settings | Same |

If your browser will not capture the mouse (some embedded pages block it), the camera still follows the mouse, keeps turning while the cursor rests at the left or right edge, and the arrow keys look around.

## Missions

Stand in a contact's marker and press **E** (they are lettered on the map):

- **J: A Car for Jax.** Steal a red Torque Falcon and deliver it clean. Lose the cops before you pull in.
- **N: Ghost Lanes.** A four-car street race around the avenues. First place or nothing.
- **D: False Kings.** Clear a gang yard in Grave Frequency, grab the ledger, then shake off the police.

The gun shop (A) sells weapons, ammo and armor. Veyra Lofts (S) is your safehouse: press E there to rest and save.

## Cheats

Type these during play: `TOOLUP` (all weapons), `PATCHUP` (health and armor), `COOLDOWN` (clear wanted level), `HEATWAVE` (more stars), `VANTARROW` (sports car), `NIGHTSHIFT` (22:00), `SUNUP` (09:00).

## Camera framing

- **On foot (standard):** character centered, camera about 4 m back and 2 m up, tilted down slightly so the street fills the lower part of the screen.
- **Close shoulder:** camera about 2.5 m back over the right shoulder, character on the left of the screen. Aiming always switches to this kind of view, tighter.
- **Chase:** camera about 6 m behind a coupe and 2 m up, tilted down about nine degrees, so the horizon sits near the top third and the road fills the lower half under the car. It follows the car exactly and only the turns swing it, so speed never drags the camera back.

## Performance

Graphics quality is picked from your GPU (Low, Medium, High, Ultra) and can be changed in Pause > Settings. The game also lowers or raises its internal resolution on the fly to stay smooth. On a Mac laptop expect the High preset; on an NVIDIA RTX PC, Ultra (with ambient occlusion and 4K shadows).

## Honest notes

- All art is original and generated by code: no models, textures, names or music from any other game. People are sculpted as smooth skinned meshes and cars are lofted bodies, so they look clean and stylized rather than photo-real.
- It was tested in headless Chrome with software rendering (screenshots in `screenshots/`), including scripted play-throughs of all three missions, police chases and a randomized stress session. It has not been played by a person on real hardware yet, so handling and difficulty may need tuning.

## Rebuild from source

The `source` folder has everything: `npm install`, then `node build.mjs` writes `dist/NeonSyndicate3D.html` (add `--dev` for a readable build). `test/multishot.mjs` drives the game in headless Chrome for screenshots.
